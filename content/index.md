I am Godfrey Yang, with a background in mechatronics engineering and formal training in cybersecurity.
This site is where I publish technical notes, walkthroughs, and study material that reflect how I approach hands-on learning.

## Background

- Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Mechatronics
- Advanced Diploma in Cyber Security

## What I work on

I am most interested in practical security work: understanding systems, breaking problems into clear steps, and documenting the result in a way that others can follow.
That includes:

- lab writeups and challenge walkthroughs
- tooling and command-line workflows
- security concepts and revision notes
- certification study summaries

## Why this site exists

I use this website as both a portfolio and a working notebook.
It helps me capture what I learn, show how I think through technical problems, and build a body of writing that is useful to revisit over time.
